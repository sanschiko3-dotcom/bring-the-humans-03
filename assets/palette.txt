make.fun colours

make.fun comes in three modes. Light is the default, and readers can switch to Dark or Night.
Magenta is the one accent. It fills the mark and the one main action of a screen. Light uses a deeper magenta on screen.
A reward figure is set in Magenta ink: the primary ink in Light, a lighter pink in Dark and Night so it stays readable.
The logo files in this kit keep the brand magenta, #C41C83, in every mode.
